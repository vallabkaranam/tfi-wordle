# TFI Wordle Outreach Pack

Last updated: April 6, 2026

## 1. Outreach Principles

- Ask for a `small pilot`, not a partnership
- Lead with `their audience`, not your product
- Show one mockup or live link immediately
- Keep the first message under 140 words when possible
- Never combine a product ask and a job ask in the same message

## 2. Asset Checklist Before Sending

Every serious outreach should include at least 2 of these:

- live demo link
- 15-30 second Loom or screen recording
- one custom mockup for that target
- one-line explanation of the audience benefit

For top-tier targets, send all 4.

## 3. Core Angles

### Angle A: Show or podcast segment

`I built a quick movie challenge your host and guest can play live on camera.`

### Angle B: Release-week fan activation

`I can turn one film into a challenge fans can play and repost during the promo cycle.`

### Angle C: Creator challenge

`Your audience can try the same movie challenge after your Reel/Story and compare scores.`

### Angle D: Media-tech collaboration

`I build lightweight audience-engagement tools for film and media teams.`

Use Angle D in a separate thread if you want to open the door to freelance, internship, or technical collaboration.

## 10. Good vs Bad Messaging

### Good

- `I made a 60-second challenge your guest can play live`
- `I can mock up a branded release-week version`
- `Your audience can try the same challenge and repost scores`

### Bad

- `This could go super viral`
- `Please promote my app`
- `Can you also help me get a job`
- `I built AI for media and gaming and entertainment and community`

## 11. First-Call Agenda

If someone replies yes, keep the first call simple:

1. Ask how they currently handle audience engagement or promo segments
2. Show the game in under 2 minutes
3. Offer one specific use case for them
4. Ask what would make it useful enough to test
5. End with a tiny next step: custom mockup, internal forward, or pilot

## 12. Objections and Responses

### `We already do games`

That is good news. This is not replacing your format; it is a lighter digital extension and a shareable fan version.

### `We are too busy right now`

Offer to make a custom mockup tied to one live promo cycle so the team can react asynchronously.

### `How is this different from a quiz?`

It is:

- daily and repeatable
- social/shareable
- fandom-status driven
- adaptable to partner campaigns

### `How do we know people will care?`

Offer a tiny pilot with one host, one creator, or one film and define success as:

- clickthrough
- start rate
- share rate
- comments/reposts

## 13. What To Send With The Message

For `shows/podcasts`:

- live demo
- 20-second guest-segment mockup

For `studios`:

- live demo
- one branded release-week mockup
- one paragraph on what metrics you can track

For `creators`:

- live demo
- custom story or reel mockup

## 14. Personalization Notes

Before each send, change:

- one recent example from their feed or show
- one reason the format matches their audience
- one concrete low-friction ask

That difference is what separates outreach from spam.
