# TFI Wordle Mockup Briefs

Last updated: April 6, 2026

These are the first 3 mockups to create before outreach goes out.

The goal is not polished design perfection. The goal is to make the pitch tangible in under 10 seconds.

## Mockup 1: Suma Adda / Guest Challenge

### Purpose

Show how TFI Wordle becomes a `segment`, not just a website.

### What to mock up

- A hero visual with:
- `Suma Adda Movie Challenge`
- `Guest vs Host`
- `Can you crack the movie in 6 tries?`

- A phone screen or laptop frame showing:
- Telugu mode selected
- a nearly solved grid
- the share/result CTA visible

- A bottom caption:
- `Play after the interview`

### Suggested share link

`[base-url]/?lang=te&partner=suma_adda&campaign=guest_challenge`

### What this mockup should imply

- this is clip-friendly
- this is easy to explain on camera
- this extends the episode after the video ends

## Mockup 3: Creator / Instagram Story Challenge

### Purpose

Show how creators can use the game as a `challenge post`, not a random link.

### What to mock up

- 9:16 Story-style layout
- top text:
- `I got this in 3/6`
- `Can you beat me?`

- a cropped game/result visual
- a clear CTA button:
- `Try the challenge`

- optional sticker-style callouts:
- `Only real Telugu movie lovers`
- `Send me your score`

### Suggested share link

`[base-url]/?lang=te&partner=creator_name&campaign=story_challenge`

### What this mockup should imply

- this belongs on Instagram
- this encourages replies and reposts
- this fits meme pages and creator loops

## Quick Creation Rules

- Keep text large and scannable
- Use Telugu-first framing
- Show one obvious CTA
- Do not clutter the mockup with every feature
- Make sure the game grid and share result are visible

## Minimum Deliverables

For each mockup, create:

1. one static image
2. one 10-20 second screen recording or Loom
3. one partner-specific URL

## Suggested Creation Order

1. Creator / Story mockup
2. Suma Adda segment mockup
3. Studio release-week mockup

This order is fastest because the creator/story version is the simplest to produce and easiest to reuse.
