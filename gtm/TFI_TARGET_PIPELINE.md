- Start where the format already makes sense
- Ask for a small pilot, not a big partnership

## Priority 4: Creator Buckets

Do not outreach to "influencers" as a generic category. Prioritize creators who already do one of these:

- Telugu movie commentary
- Telugu meme/reaction content
- Telugu interview clips
- diaspora Telugu lifestyle with strong movie fandom

Qualifying signs:

- Their audience comments with movie references already
- They use Stories and Broadcast Channels consistently
- They have repostable community participation formats

Disqualifying signs:

- Audience is not movie-native
- Feed is too broad and brand-safe in a way that makes game content feel random
- They only do one-way celebrity news reposts and never audience prompts

## How To Reach Each Type

### Shows, podcasts, outlets

Use this order:

1. official business/contact page if available
2. producer/editor LinkedIn
3. Instagram DM to official account with a 1-line hook and demo link

### Studios

Use this order:

1. PR or digital marketing lead via LinkedIn
2. official studio Instagram DM
3. agency or campaign contact if a release is active

### Creators

Use this order:

1. Creator Marketplace if available
2. business email in bio
3. Instagram DM with a custom mockup

## Rules For The First 25 Outreaches

- 10 should be shows/outlets
- 8 should be studios or studio-adjacent promo teams
- 5 should be meme or movie-native creators
- 2 should be diaspora/community groups

Do not send the same note to all 25.

Every note should change:

- the opening line
- the example use case
- the exact ask
- the proof asset attached
