# TFI Wordle GTM Execution Plan

Last updated: April 6, 2026

## 1. Core Bet

TFI Wordle should not launch as "another game."

It should launch as:

- The daily movie-flex ritual for Telugu cinema fans
- A reusable audience-engagement format for movie promotions, podcasts, and creator content

That means the GTM is:

- `B2C`: daily fandom habit
- `B2B2C`: promo partners use the game to create moments, then their audience comes into the game

## 2. Positioning

### Consumer positioning

`Guess the movie in 6 tries using hero, heroine, director, music, producer, and year clues.`

Why this works:

- It is instantly legible
- It rewards fandom knowledge
- It is naturally challengeable with friends
- It generates spoiler-light sharing, which is the same mechanic that made Wordle spread

### Partner positioning

`A plug-and-play movie challenge format for release-week promos, podcasts, interviews, and fan campaigns.`

Why this works:

- Shows need recurring segments
- Studios need interactive promo ideas
- Creators need formats that create comments, duets, DMs, and story reposts

## 3. Best Wedge

### Phase 1 wedge

Go `Telugu-first`.

Do not lead with "all Indian cinema."

Reasons:

- Telugu promo culture already embraces games, chaos, and fan-interaction segments
- Telugu audiences are unusually active across YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp, and diaspora communities
- You already have a culturally specific product hook

### Beachhead audiences

1. Movie-promo interview ecosystems
2. Studio digital and PR teams
3. Telugu meme pages and movie-native creators
4. Telugu diaspora communities

## 4. Primary ICPs

### ICP 1: Promo shows and podcasts

What they want:

- A reusable segment
- Better clips
- More comments and fan participation

Pitch:

`I built a 60-second movie challenge your guest can play live on camera.`

### ICP 2: Studios and production houses

What they want:

- Release-week engagement
- Fan activation
- A branded interactive asset that feels newer than a poster drop

Pitch:

`I can spin up a branded release-week movie challenge for your film, page, or promo tour.`

### ICP 3: Movie-native creators

What they want:

- Fresh content formats
- Audience participation
- Something their followers can try and repost

Pitch:

`I made a custom movie challenge your followers can try after watching your Reel or Story.`

### ICP 4: Diaspora orgs and community groups

What they want:

- Cultural engagement
- Family-friendly activities
- Telugu-language pride and participation

Pitch:

`This can be a daily Telugu movie challenge for your community, youth events, or cultural weekends.`

## 5. Distribution Strategy

### Track A: Partner-led seeding

Goal: borrow distribution from people who already own Telugu attention.

Targets:

- Suma-style interview formats
- Aha-style celebrity game formats
- Telugu interview/news channels
- Studio-owned digital handles

### Track B: Creator-led challenge loops

Goal: convert the game into a creator prompt.

Formats:

- "I got this in 3/6, can you beat me?"
- "Only true Telugu movie lovers can get this"
- "Guest vs host"
- "Release-week movie special"

### Track C: Community loops

Goal: create dense, high-trust spread instead of waiting for broad algorithmic luck.

Channels:

- WhatsApp groups
- Telegram communities
- Telugu Discord/Reddit pockets
- Diaspora associations
- Alumni and college Telugu groups

### Track D: Owned social

Goal: make the game itself look alive every day.

Daily content ideas:

- Today’s result pattern
- Funny near-miss guesses
- "Only OGs know this one"
- Friday hard-mode puzzle
- Weekend "guest challenge" clip

## 6. Why Instagram Still Matters

Instagram should be treated as a `distribution layer`, not the only product home.

Reasons:

- Meta expanded Instagram Creator Marketplace to India on February 21, 2024, which makes structured brand-creator outreach more realistic: <https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/creator-marketplace-for-brands-and-creators-to-collaborate-on-instagram/>
- Broadcast Channels give creators a direct recurring fan-notification lane: <https://about.fb.com/news/2023/02/instagram-broadcast-channels-creators-deepen-connections-with-followers/>
- Trial Reels let creators test formats with non-followers before fully pushing them: <https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/>

Practical takeaway:

- Pitch creators a `Story/Reel + challenge link` flow, not a generic "please post my app"

## 7. Product Requirements To Match GTM

Before heavier outreach, the product should clearly support:

1. Easy sharing
2. Mobile-native sharing
3. Trackable referral links
4. Re-playable random challenge links
5. Branded variants for partners

Current work in repo now supports:

- native share on supported browsers
- attributed share links
- language-aware share links
- random-seed share links

Still needed next:

- image-based result cards for Instagram Stories
- branded landing-page variants for partner campaigns
- persistent analytics store instead of log-only telemetry
- very short intro/demo video

## 8. Metrics That Actually Matter

Your previous GTM work got strong feedback because it was specific on ICP, proof, and metrics. Reuse that discipline here.

Track these:

- `Partner response rate`: replies / outreach sent
- `Meeting rate`: calls / replies
- `Pilot conversion`: pilots / meetings
- `Start rate`: game starts / landing sessions
- `Completion rate`: completed games / starts
- `Share rate`: share actions / completed games
- `Replay rate`: random replays / users
- `Return-next-day rate`: next-day daily opens / daily users

## 9. Traps To Avoid

### Trap 1: Pitching "virality"

Nobody serious buys "this could go viral."
They buy:

- engagement
- participation
- retention
- reusable format

### Trap 2: Mixing the product ask with the career ask

If you want to work with a studio, do that in a separate note.

Bad:

- "Please use my product and also hire me"

Good:

- one note for a product pilot
- one note for media-tech collaboration or internship-style help

### Trap 3: Going too broad too early

Do not open with:

- Bollywood
- Tamil
- all India
- all creators

Start with the densest surface area: Telugu promo + Telugu fandom.

### Trap 4: Outreach without a packaged artifact

Do not ask for a call with only words.

Always have:

- live link
- one-sentence pitch
- one mockup or Loom
- specific ask

## 10. 14-Day Sprint

### Days 1-3

- finalize share flow and campaign tracking
- create 3 mocked partner concepts
- prepare outreach list of 25 targets

### Days 4-6

- send first 10 tailored outreaches
- post first owned social challenge assets
- collect response patterns and objections

### Days 7-10

- send follow-ups
- send second batch of 10 improved outreaches
- make one custom pilot mockup for the warmest target

### Days 11-14

- try to convert 2-3 calls into 1 pilot
- tighten metrics instrumentation
- prepare a simple pilot case-study page
